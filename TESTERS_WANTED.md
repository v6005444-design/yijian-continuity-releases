# Testers Wanted — Yijian Continuity Technical Beta

We are looking for **3–5 Windows users** who already have a long-running AI relationship or workflow and care about what gets lost when models or platforms change.

This is not a download-count experiment.

The question is:

> Can a user-owned Partner state preserve enough identity, working style, preferences, permissions, cancellations, provenance, and unresolved unknowns that the restored result still feels meaningfully continuous?

## Good fit

You may be a good tester if:

- you have used ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Character.AI, Replika, or another AI system over time;
- losing accumulated context or relationship/workflow continuity would matter to you;
- you use Windows;
- you are comfortable trying an early Technical Beta;
- you are willing to give a blunt verdict, including “not continuous”.

## What you do

1. Read the [5-step Pilot 1 Quick Start](PILOT_1_QUICKSTART.md).
2. Download the official Technical Beta.
3. Bring in evidence from your existing AI relationship/workflow.
4. Review, export, restore, and verify the Partner state.
5. Submit a high-level continuity verdict using the **Pilot 1 continuity feedback** issue template.

## What we want to learn

We care about:

- Did the technical restore work?
- Did the restored result feel meaningfully continuous, partly continuous, or not continuous?
- What important thing was preserved?
- What important thing was lost or wrong?
- Would you trust this workflow enough to use it again before switching models/platforms?

## Privacy

Do not publicly share:

- full chat exports;
- Partner Bundle contents;
- private memory/relationship text;
- credentials or recovery codes;
- personal local paths;
- private information about other people.

High-level descriptions are enough.

## Current status

Technical Public Beta, not Stable.
Windows only.
Local-first.
No remote product telemetry by default.
Current local Partner data / Partner Bundles are not application-layer encrypted.
